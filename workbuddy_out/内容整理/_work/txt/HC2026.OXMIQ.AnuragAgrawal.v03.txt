SOFTWARE
HBF spec — the software constraints
•
Access chunks for max BW: 64 KB 
reads, 1 MB writes (64 KB aligned)
•
HBF read/write through DMA — not 
designed for the GPU cache hierarchy
•
Separate memory management when 
HBF and HBM are used together
•
Power-on data retention ~24h at 85 °C 
— host-managed lifecycle required
•
Careful host mgmt. to reach ~10-yr life 
or 100% endurance consumption
•
Scratchpad SRAM cannot read/write 
the NAND blocks directly
Read-optimized, write-constrained, host-managed — placement is a software problem

CLOSE
Takeaways — bargain or trap?
1
Judge any memory by one number: $mem = β·max(C, I·b/α); 
the figure of merit is (β/α)·b —OR technology times the workload.
2
HBF wins in ONE zone — low bandwidth demand (I·b): MoE at small B / low I, and 
long-context sparse KV. Dense and high-B stay on HBM.
3
For HBF to matter more: read BW must rise (α↑), the $/GB gap must stay wide (β↓ vs 
HBM), and write endurance + write BW/latency must be handled.
HBF: A precision instrument, not a hammer — bargain in its zone, trap outside it

4
Software prerequisite: an HBF allocator/backend, placement policy, async prefetch, 
and endurance telemetry — today’s vLLM paths target CPU/LMCache, not HBF.
