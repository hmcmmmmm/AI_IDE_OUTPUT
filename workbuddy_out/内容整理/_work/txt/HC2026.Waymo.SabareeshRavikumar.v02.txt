p. 12
Dataflow principles
Low HW Complexity
Static shapes and 
deterministic control flow:
●No caching, branches, or 
register allocation
●Expose full capability as 
spatial hardware threads 
(e.g. compute, DMA)
Mega-Instructions
Single thread execution over 
O(1000) cycles:
●How hardware FSM iterates 
over tensor dimensions with 
high flexibility
●Synchronization with other 
threads (SPMC, MPSC)
Thread Sync
Two streaming primitives:
●FIFO: Enforces strict ordering 
and propagates backpressure
●Memory with semaphores: 
Coarse grained with relaxed 
ordering
